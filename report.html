UT Austin campus Kestrel field-data report

Status: Descriptive campus field analysis. This is not a crash model or a validation of modeled shade.

Dataset and exclusions

36 CSV files contained 50 sessions and 5,933 readings. Three sessions from reported defective instrument 3080884 were excluded. The analysis retains 47 sessions, 5,563 readings, and two instruments. Provisional note-based groups are Sun (10), Shade (16), Mixed (11), and Unknown (10).

Processing

The analysis groups readings by instrument serial number and recorded session name, then calculates a mean for each retained session. The map shows 45 of the 47 retained sessions with usable recorded coordinates. These are mapped sessions, not a count of independently sampled sites.

Measurements

Overall means, weighting each retained session equally: air temperature 92.10 °F; globe temperature 103.71 °F; wet bulb temperature 78.35 °F; wet bulb globe temperature 86.02 °F; relative humidity 56.49%; wind speed 2.31 mph; heat index 103.65 °F; dew point 73.46 °F; natural wet bulb 80.33 °F; thermal work limit 173.15 W/m².

Analysis outputs

The executed notebook includes distributions for ten original metrics, within-session trajectories, globe-versus-air temperature scatterplots, three-dimensional plots of temperature and wind, and a location map. View the four plots and the session table.

Limitations

  • Exposure labels are provisional and require review against field notes. Three notes containing “no shade” are currently classified as Mixed. Review the notes before comparing groups. Recorded coordinates should be checked against instrument placement before matching a modeled shadow boundary.
  • Instrument 3080884 was excluded following a reported malfunction. 26 retained sessions were flagged by one or more automated checks for duration, logging gaps, missing core readings, or globe-temperature drift. These flags did not trigger further exclusions.
  • Some sessions may not have reached thermal equilibrium.
  • Sessions are observational and may be correlated in time and space; temperature, shade, wind, surface and location are potential confounders.
  • Repeated five-second readings within a session are not independent replicates; reported tables use session means.
  • These observations do not establish a causal shade effect.
  • Location markers are original, unverified coordinates; points are not an interpolated continuous campus heat map.
