UT Austin campus Kestrel field-data report

Status: Summary extracted from the executed notebook's final Markdown report. This is not a separately authored or approved departmental report.

Dataset and exclusions

36 CSV files contained 50 sessions and 5,933 readings. Three sessions from reported defective instrument 3080884 were excluded. The analysis retains 47 sessions, 5,563 readings, and two instruments. Exposure groups: Sun (10), Shade (16), Mixed (11), Unknown (10).

Measurements

Overall means, weighting each retained session equally: air temperature 92.10 °F; globe temperature 103.71 °F; wet bulb temperature 78.35 °F; wet bulb globe temperature 86.02 °F; relative humidity 56.49%; wind speed 2.31 mph; heat index 103.65 °F; dew point 73.46 °F; natural wet bulb 80.33 °F; thermal work limit 173.15 W/m².

Analysis outputs

The executed notebook includes distributions for ten original metrics, within-session trajectories, globe-versus-air temperature scatterplots, three-dimensional plots of temperature and wind, and a location map. The complete plots have not yet been deployed to this site.

Limitations

  • Exposure labels are provisional; notes, photo timestamps and instrument placement require independent validation.
  • Instrument 3080884 was excluded following a reported malfunction. Other quality issues were flagged but not excluded.
  • Some sessions may not have reached thermal equilibrium.
  • Sessions are observational and may be correlated in time and space; temperature, shade, wind, surface and location are potential confounders.
  • Repeated five-second readings within a session are not independent replicates; reported tables use session means.
  • These observations do not establish a causal shade effect.
  • Location markers are original, unverified coordinates; points are not an interpolated continuous campus heat map.
